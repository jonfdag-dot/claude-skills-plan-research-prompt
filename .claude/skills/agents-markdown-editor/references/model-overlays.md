## Applying a lead

Confirm the actual directive is an unnecessary effort obligation or earlier-model compensation rather than an acceptance criterion, explicit preference or proactive safeguard. Use [cut-classes.md](cut-classes.md) for combinations, source standards and counterevidence. A supported discretionary cut still needs operator selection unless already covered by a direct bounded request.

Generic “double-check” may be deleted as overlap; it need not become another test. Lack of a generic verification sentence is not a defect by itself, including at lower effort. Preserve a named unresolved acceptance check when one exists. Capitals, absence of incidents and model-default expectations never establish a deletion fact.
